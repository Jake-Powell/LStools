# Fake

A fake dataset of student information containing maths grades at KS1 -
KS4 and some demographics.

## Usage

``` r
fake_data
```

## Format

\## \`fake_data\` A data frame with 10,000 rows and 7 columns:

- FSM:

  Does the student recieve Free School Meals (FSM).

- IDACI:

  What IDACI quintie does the student belong to.

- Gender:

  What Gender is the student.

- KS1:

  KS1 maths outcome.

- KS2:

  KS2 maths outcome.

- GCSE:

  GCSE mathematics grade.

- A_level:

  Does the student study maths and/or further maths at A level.
